    Questions

    Common questions

    How do I take it?

    One 10 g sachet a day, stirred into 150–200 ml of cold or room-temperature water, juice or a smoothie. Avoid very hot liquids. Pick a fixed moment in your day and keep it — consistency matters more than timing.

    How long until I notice anything?

    Collagen supplementation is a long game, not a week. We built the 90-day journey precisely because a fair trial is a full three-month routine. We don't promise a specific result or a timeline — anyone who does is selling you certainty they don't have.

    Is it halal? Is it marine or bovine?

    The formula is built on marine (fish-derived) hydrolyzed collagen peptides, as printed on the box. For certification documents specific to your batch, message us on WhatsApp and we'll send the current product file.

    Can I take it while pregnant, breastfeeding, or on medication?

    Please check with your doctor or pharmacist first. This is a food supplement, not a medicine, and it shouldn't replace a varied diet or any prescribed treatment. Not intended for children.

    Does the skin snapshot measure my collagen?

    No. It reads visual characteristics of the photo you upload — brightness, tone evenness, surface detail and colour warmth — entirely inside your browser. It is a wellness and personalisation tool, not a diagnosis, and lighting alone can change the numbers.

    Where do you deliver, and how do I pay?

    Across Oman, typically within 1–3 working days, free in Muscat on orders over OMR 20. Cash on delivery, bank transfer or a card payment link — confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp. GCC shipping is arranged case by case.

    Can my pharmacy stock Noorala?

    Yes — that's the Partners section of this site. We supply directly from Muscat and include the in-pharmacy skin analysis activation, retail kit and staff training.
